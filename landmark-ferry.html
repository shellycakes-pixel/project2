Staten Island Ferry

Photo by Praswin Prakashan.

The Staten Island Ferry links Manhattan and Staten Island across New York Harbor and offers free rides with stunning skyline views.

About the Landmark

Built

The ferry service began in 1817, when the Richmond Turnpike Company started operating boats between Manhattan and Staten Island. After several mergers and sales, the New York City government took control in 1905.

Today

The Staten Island Ferry remains one of the busiest ferry services in the United States, serving millions of passengers every year. Beyond transportation, it is a scenic experience for visitors looking for harbor and Statue of Liberty views.

Directions

For a trip starting in Manhattan, go to Whitehall Terminal at 4 South Street. The Staten Island terminal is St. George Terminal at 1 Bay Street.
