Yankee Stadium

Photo by Malcolm Hill

Yankee Stadium, located in the Bronx, is the home field of the New York Yankees baseball team and New York City FC soccer team.

About Yankee Stadium

Built

The original Yankee Stadium opened in April 1923. It became legendary for baseball milestones, World Series games, and major events in American sports history. It was home to the New York Yankees for generations and became known as "The House That Ruth Built" after Babe Ruth.

Today

The original stadium was replaced in 2009 by a new stadium with the same name. The new 46,543-seat stadium pays homage to the original through its exterior and playing field design while adding modern amenities.

Directions

Yankee Stadium is located at 1 East 161st Street in the Bronx. Check your ticket for the correct entrance.
