Empire State Building

Photo by Trace Hudson.

The Empire State Building is an Art Deco skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan that once held the title of world’s tallest building.

About the Landmark

Built

Constructed in a record 1 year and 45 days from 1930–1931, the 102-story tower was designed by Shreve, Lamb & Harmon Associates. It opened on May 1, 1931, with President Hoover pressing a button in Washington, D.C., that turned on the building's lights.

Today

Its history, iconic silhouette, and observation decks make it a global tourist destination and an official city and national landmark. Millions of people visit it every year.

Directions

The observatory visitor entrance is at 20 West 34th Street, between Fifth and Sixth Avenues in Manhattan.
